Pocket Tank CYD

The real game and pixel renderer, running locally in your browser. Choose Normal, Hard or Creative on your first visit.

Loading the game…

Mouse or touchscreen: tap a fish to open its summary; tap, drag and hold directly on the screen. Tap near the water surface to feed, or choose Place food and tap anywhere in the tank to drop food from the surface at that horizontal position. Choose Fullscreen to enlarge the game; Escape or Exit fullscreen returns to this page. Touch hints show tap/drag positions. Use Menu → Games for mini-games and stories. Shortcuts unlock after choosing a first-run profile. The page can scroll normally outside the game screen.

Your browser save

All profiles, Bank, aquariums and preferences stay in this browser on this site. Autosaved every 5 seconds, after actions, when hidden and on exit. Keep an export: clearing site data, private browsing or browser eviction can remove saves. Only one tab can play at a time.

Checking local storage…

Browser simulation, not a device connection. No Wi-Fi, live weather, sensor, LED, touch calibration, SD card, USB or firmware flashing. Hardware-only actions are unavailable. No credentials are requested and no saves are sent to a server. This does not synchronize with your CYD. Browser save exports are complete browser archives, not device backup files. The “+25 dollars” desktop debug shortcut is intentionally omitted.
