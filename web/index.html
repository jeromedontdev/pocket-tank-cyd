A life of its own.

Meet your fish, grow a family, decorate their home, and watch a calm pixel world unfold on your Cheap Yellow Display. No cloud AI. No subscriptions. Just your tank.

Originally a hardware port of Pocket Tank, CYD has grown into a distinct aquarium game: permanent game modes, a shared multi-tank collection, inherited genetics, illustrated stories and eighteen games. Its original simulation and authors remain credited.

No CYD yet? Play here.

The browser simulator runs the actual game, with mouse and touchscreen controls and its own locally saved aquarium. No board, USB cable or account is needed. Your browser game is separate from a physical CYD; it does not automatically synchronize device saves.

Fullscreen keeps the controls handy. Choose Place food and tap a feeding column; Touch hints show your taps. These browser controls require 2.1.1. Version 2.1.2 also fixes the fish-card padlock, sponge/scissors and DONE controls: lock bare-hand cleaning/trimming without disabling explicit tools. The public game is compiled from the latest stable release source, not the ESP32 binary. Its release tag and source commit are shown above the game and checked against the USB installer's version before deployment.

In 2.1.3, a simpler Clock/Weather hub groups Face, Colors, Text / Effects, Time / Zone and Weather, with one Show Clock preview. Weather has its own Small/Medium/Large text control, plus simple condition icons. BACK returns one level, and city removal requires confirmation. Live weather remains a CYD hardware feature; the browser does not contact weather services.

Progress stays in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, private browsing or browser storage eviction can remove it. Hardware-only features are not emulated.

Aquarium art polish: 2.2.2

A more crafted little aquarium.

The polish is now inside the tank: twenty ornaments and sand scenery pieces gain rounded shapes, material-specific shading and quieter highlights. Wood grain, brass fittings, faceted crystals, pearl sheen and ceramic details sit among jade seagrass and folded sword leaves. Your owned decorations, placement, growth, daily care and saves stay yours.

Interface art polish: 2.2.1

Small details. A more finished world.

Shaded menu icons, coordinated game thumbnails and faceted medals bring a consistent cozy pixel-art style to the interface. Clear silhouettes, gentle highlights and restrained aqua, gold and rose colors stay crisp on the CYD and in your browser. Your aquarium and daily care are unchanged.

Care-first progression: 2.2.0

A little care. A reason to return.

Feed, connect with a fish, and tend or quietly watch your reef. Three flexible daily activities offer up to 100 sand dollars and 50 XP, with no streak penalties or missed-day debt. The budget aims at weeks of short daily visits, not grinding.

Games are optional fun: unlimited play, continued Challenge records and medals, but just five shared bonus dollars a day and no Journal XP. Existing progress stays yours.

Settings refresh: 2.1.3

Clearer menus. Weather you can read.

Clock/Weather now has one clean hub and one preview action. Weather text has a visible Small/Medium/Large control, with simple icons for actual cached conditions. City search goes back one level, and forgetting a city needs confirmation.

New in 2.1.0

Your scene. Your clock. Comfortable light.

Available in Pocket Tank CYD 2.1.0, alongside all the game features below.

Three scene-detail levels

Normal size and Balanced detail are the defaults. Choose Simple for stationary real seagrass and decorations without helper creatures, bubbles or ambient effects; their benefits continue. Enhanced keeps smooth animations. Size and detail are independent.

Populated-device samples at either size: about 20 FPS Simple, 10 FPS Balanced and 8-9 FPS Enhanced. Results depend on your aquarium.

Jumbo clocks that keep their style

Tiny also joins the size choices. Clock/Weather > Text / Effects has separate Small/Medium/Large controls for date/AM-PM/seconds and weather/activity. Captions wrap and sizes fit down when necessary to prevent overlap at either screen resolution.

Make the time easier to read from across the room. Jumbo respects Minimal, Digital, Analog, Neon, Flip, Binary and Orbit designs, plus your colors, date/seconds options and enabled weather captions. Large, Medium and Small remain available.

Quieter in a dark room

Room-light dimming now controls both the screen and rear LED, with much stronger low-light reduction: about 27% output at half room light, 8% at a quarter, and 2% in darkness. The screen keeps a small usable minimum, while filtering softens brief shadows. Pagination buttons use clear arrows instead of page labels.

New in 2.0.0

Deeper adventures. Still your quiet tank.

These additions are included in Pocket Tank CYD 2.0.0. The flasher shows the exact published version it will install.

The Lost Current

An optional six-chapter, 18-encounter story RPG. Choose a fish guide and use courage, curiosity, or kindness to find your path to one of three endings.

Your fish's bonds, inherited traits, family legacy, decorations, visitors, and past adventures can offer help. No fish leaves home or gets hurt, no purchases are required, and no deadlines or background simulation change the aquarium you already love.

Eighteen games, three pages

Lighthouse, Shell Slide, Sonar Sweep, Tide 2048, Harbor Stack and Echo Reef join the original twelve. Help, pause, saved records, medals, and carefully saved rewards are included.

Clearer menus & finishing touches

Illustrated destinations and fish information, flatter menus and consistent BACK navigation. Choose Normal, Creative or Hard on first run; changing mode requires a full wipe. Money and progression follow every aquarium; the global Bank transfers fish and their histories.

Ready to give your CYD a little world?

  1. Check that your board is the supported ESP32-2432S028 CYD.
  2. Use desktop Chrome or Edge and a USB data cable.
  3. Open the flasher and choose a save-preserving update for an existing tank, or a factory installation for a new board.

Back up first. Factory installation overwrites saved tanks and settings. A save-preserving update is a separate operation with its own board/layout checks and confirmation. Nothing flashes from this home page.
