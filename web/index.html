A life of its own.

Raise fish, breed new generations, trade at the harbor market and decorate a calm pixel aquarium on your Cheap Yellow Display. No cloud AI. No subscriptions. Just your tank.

Originally a hardware port of Pocket Tank, CYD has grown into a distinct aquarium game: a serene breeding-and-trading game with inherited genetics, one currency (doubloons), up to eight aquariums, eighteen games, stories and a small casino. Its original simulation and authors remain credited.

What is in the tank today

More than fish on a screen

Every screenshot below comes from the current firmware's actual renderer. MENU opens Fish, Tank, Market, Games, Casino and Help; the cog in the corner opens Settings.

A calm, living aquarium

Fish that swim, rest and grow

You start with two Tetras. Swipe down from the top of the glass to drop food and tap a fish to open its summary. Fish ease into rest spots, hover near cover and grow from fry to juvenile, adult and elder.

Ten varieties unlock as you go: Tetra, Guppy, Clownfish, Betta, Harborfin, Angel, Puffer, Jellyfish, Stingray and Shark. Rare visitors occasionally pass the glass, and seasonal accents follow the hemisphere of your weather city.

Get to know each fish

Care, genetics and family

A fish's summary shows Trust, Energy, Fullness, Calm and Vigor, its genes and worth, and the decoration it LOVES. Keep that favorite piece in the tank and calm recovers 30% faster while the fish grows 10% faster; without it the fish stays a little restless and grows 10% slower. Calm is cultivated: a newcomer settles only part way, and every point of trust you earn with meals and gentle holds lets it settle further.

Each fish carries two marking alleles, two body-color alleles and four recessive traits. Two healthy adults of the same variety can breed, and fry inherit one allele of each gene from each parent, sometimes with a rare mutation. The Fish menu adds Rename (an on-screen keyboard with a SUGGEST key), Family, Genes, Life, Wardrobe and the Fish Bank.

Breed, raise and sell

A market that rewards good lines

Value is life stage × variety tier × care × genes. Grades run Common, Fine (125%), Rare (150%) and Prized (190%), and one variety is in demand each day for a 25% bonus. Market stock is plain and costs more than its resale, so profit comes from breeding and raising. A pedigree certificate shows ancestry, genes and vigor on every sale.

Collections reward finding every mutation of a variety with a gold statue for your tank. Contracts arrive every three days, and the Saturday Auction sells one prized fish to rival bidders.

Make it your aquarium

Decor, foliage, food and themes

Tank > Decor & Storage buys, places, stores and sells pieces; hold a piece in the tank to move it, or REMOVE any piece, fixed scenery included, to storage. The Lighthouse, Stone Bridge, Pagoda and Torii Gate stand as tall or wide as the castle. Ground-cover foliage and kelp are permanent sets that never need trimming, and seahorses perch on kelp, sword grass and the tops of decorations. Pellets, Flakes and Bloodworms are permanent diets that speed up growth. Themes are bought once with doubloons and stay unlocked for every tank; Original is free.

Up to eight aquariums

Every tank its own little world

Keep up to eight aquariums, each with its own residents, decor and theme. Tank Summary switches, adds and reviews tanks; Name & Identity renames a tank and picks a subtitle and an illustrated emblem, with enough of each for every tank to be unique.

Play with your fish

Games, stories and The Lost Current

Main Menu > Games lists all eighteen touch games, the Stories trips and The Lost Current RPG together in one paged list. Each game pays its score reward plus 2 doubloons for up to five rounds a day. Current Runner is a tap-to-rise dive through rock gaps, and Reef Architect is a coral logic puzzle with row and column clues. The Casino holds Fish Races, Reef Reels, Pearl Hi-Lo, a Shell Game, Treasure Dive and the Tide Wheel, with bets of 5 to 50 doubloons.

A useful desk companion

Clock faces and weather

Choose from seven clock faces: Minimal, Digital, Analog, Neon, Flip, Binary and Orbit. Analog keeps a brass dial; the others sit on rounded sea glass. Set a weather city to show live conditions in a matching weather box. Time and weather use Wi-Fi when enabled; the aquarium itself works offline. The room-light sensor and rear RGB LED are supported, and no speaker or audio setup is needed.

Your tank stays yours

Help, settings and backups

Help explains every part of the game, one topic at a time. Settings holds display, clock and weather, Wi-Fi, SD backups, aquarium care and device options. Creative mode removes prices; Hard mode adds real starvation risk. Saves stay on the board, an SD card can back up and restore the whole save, and firmware updates come from GitHub over Wi-Fi or from an SD card.

Supported boards

Two displays, the same little aquarium

CYD and ELEGOO CYD use the same firmware application. Choose your hardware profile in the USB installer; it remembers the correct display setup. Reliable automatic board detection is not available on these displays. Setup requires an explicit choice, with no preselected default; the board retains that profile across reboots and firmware updates. When using the web updater again, select the matching board so it can verify the saved profile. Factory images set that profile for new boards. Both use the same SD update and GitHub Wi-Fi channel; legacy update downloads remain available.

ELEGOO CYD needs different LCD initialization, not different touch pins. Its GPIO34 senses a battery, so automatic room-light dimming is unavailable; manual brightness, touch, games and internal tanks still work. No battery or audio application is included. CYD is a family of boards, not a guarantee that every display variant is compatible.

No CYD yet? Play here.

The browser simulator runs the actual game, with mouse and touchscreen controls and its own locally saved aquarium. No board, USB cable or account is needed. Your browser game is separate from a physical CYD; it does not automatically synchronize device saves.

The public game is compiled from the latest stable release source, not the ESP32 binary. Its release tag and source commit are shown above the game and checked against the USB installer's version before deployment. Fullscreen keeps the controls handy. Live weather, Wi-Fi updates and other hardware-only features are not emulated.

Progress stays in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, private browsing or browser storage eviction can remove it.

Ready to give your CYD a little world?

  1. Choose the CYD or ELEGOO CYD hardware profile matching your board in the flasher.
  2. Use desktop Chrome or Edge and a USB data cable.
  3. Open the flasher and choose a save-preserving update for an existing tank, or a factory installation for a new board.

Back up first. Factory installation overwrites saved tanks and settings. A save-preserving update is a separate operation with its own board/layout checks and confirmation. Nothing flashes from this home page.
