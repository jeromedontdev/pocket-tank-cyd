A life of its own.

Meet your fish, grow a family, decorate their home, and watch a calm pixel world unfold on your Cheap Yellow Display. No cloud AI. No subscriptions. Just your tank.

Originally a hardware port of Pocket Tank, CYD has grown into a distinct aquarium game: permanent game modes, a shared multi-tank collection, inherited genetics, illustrated stories and eighteen games. Its original simulation and authors remain credited.

Made for ESP32-2432S028 CYD and ELEGOO ESP32-32E 2.8-inch ILI9341V: resistive touchscreen, 4 MB flash, no PSRAM required.

Published firmware: see the latest release. The flasher uses the latest stable GitHub release. The current release includes all eight internally stored aquariums, shared inventory, pearl-mussel income, eighteen games and the audited menus below. 2.3.6 adds one shared CYD/ELEGOO application with saved hardware profiles and save-preserving USB migration. Select the actual board in the installer; legacy board-specific update downloads remain available. 2.3.5 clarifies update navigation and removes duplicate controls; GitHub Wi-Fi updating requires 2.3.2 or newer, and SD-free tank switching requires 2.3.4 or newer. Check the published version above before installing. Older releases remain available, but cannot load the expanded current save format.

New in 2.3.6

Two displays, the same little aquarium

The USB installer now offers the original CYD and the ELEGOO ESP32-32E 2.8-inch ILI9341V separately. Each has its own app, factory image, SD update and GitHub Wi-Fi channel; normal updates refuse a different board.

The ELEGOO needs different LCD initialization, not different touch pins. Its GPIO34 senses a battery, so automatic room-light dimming is unavailable; manual brightness, touch, games and internal tanks still work. No battery or audio application is included. An ESP32-32E module label alone does not identify other display boards.

New in 2.3.5

Clearer menus, easier updates

Settings > Device > Firmware Updates puts Check GitHub via Wi-Fi and Check SD Card side by side. Versions 2.3.4 and older called this entry SD Updates.

Screenshot to SD now has one home under Device; weather text size has one home under Clock/Weather > Weather. Aligned buttons, corrected clipped text and cleaner inventory panels make both Normal and Enlarged easier to use. The audit checks 434 rendered menu states across both scales.

New in 2.3.4

Eight aquariums, no card required

Buying and switching aquariums now uses internal flash. Your Fish Bank, inventory and active aquarium already save internally. Keep an existing multi-tank installation's original SD inserted for the first upgraded boot to import inactive tanks; the original files are retained.

SD stays useful for backups, screenshots and offline updates. Imported SD snapshots are historical copies, not kept in sync. Visit each aquarium to make current backups. No partition or save-schema changes.

New in 2.3.3

Keep a picture of your aquarium

Settings > Device > Screenshot to SD saves a BMP image to pocket-tank/photos/. The old duplicate under SD Backups was removed in 2.3.5; the screenshot feature remains.

Wi-Fi update mode also leaves more ESP32 memory free for GitHub's TLS certificate verification, while retaining all download checks and the separate offline SD update option.

New in 2.3.2

The next update, without the cable

After installing 2.3.2 once, open Settings > Device > Firmware Updates > Check GitHub via Wi-Fi (called SD Updates in 2.3.4 and older). The tank checks the latest stable CYD release and asks before downloading a newer version.

Certificate-verified HTTPS, an exact-release app URL, board/version/size checks and SHA-256 verification protect the download before switching boot slots. Your aquarium is saved first; factory images are never used. Keep power connected and make an SD backup first when available.

Release files are trusted downloads, not publisher-signed firmware. USB and offline SD updates remain available.

New in 2.3.1

A little leaner, a little clearer

Inventory item panels now have one explicit Back button, which cancels an unconfirmed sale without changing your items or money. The pearl mussel explains when coin growth is paused for hungry or stressed fish.

Selective size optimization and client-only TLS recover 19,312 bytes versus 2.3.0, without removing artwork, gameplay features, certificate verification or either safe-update slot. Weather and GitHub Pages HTTPS certificate validation were checked on a real CYD. Existing aquarium saves and layouts remain compatible.

New in 2.3.0

Make room for more little treasures

Shop > Inventory lets you buy duplicate decorations, store pieces without selling, and move them between aquariums. Shared storage holds 64 pieces; each tank can display eight extra basic ornaments with independent positions and front/back depth. Complex growing decorations remain one placed copy of each type per tank.

Daily care now pays 125 sand dollars and 50 XP. Claim all three tasks to welcome a free pearl mussel: it grows one coin per ten healthy running minutes, up to 15 per day and 15 banked. Collect through Shop or Inventory. No offline earnings; it usually rests, with a gentle crawl along the sand roughly every six minutes.

Games pay one dollar per qualifying saved round, up to five per game per day — 90 across all eighteen. Challenge and Relaxed share each game's allowance across all tanks; no game XP. Established fish no longer reopen obsolete “new fry” welcomes, and a failed welcome save stays open for retry.

Existing layouts and purchases migrate automatically. Back up before upgrading: schema 18 saves cannot be read by older firmware. Use Update / keep aquarium, not Factory reset.

New in 2.2.7

More room in the frame budget

Fuller use of existing LCD DMA buffers, reused fish/helper math and clipped clock spans improve performance without reducing artwork, changing care or increasing the 40 MHz SPI clock. The optimizations apply across display scales, scene details and gameplay modes; LCD transfer savings are CYD-only.

A five-fish RAM copy of a decorated aquarium with its clock enabled measured 10.79 FPS on a real CYD in Normal / Balanced over 1,200 frames. The slowest 120-frame window was 10.22 FPS, including a 5 ms/frame allowance for normal services.

This is a bounded diagnostic, not the full gameplay/network loop or a guaranteed minimum. Ordinary four-fish monitoring still included sub-10 FPS windows. Both scales and all scene details retain matching reference images, with no extra image buffers or save-format changes.

No CYD yet? Play here.

The browser simulator runs the actual game, with mouse and touchscreen controls and its own locally saved aquarium. No board, USB cable or account is needed. Your browser game is separate from a physical CYD; it does not automatically synchronize device saves.

Fullscreen keeps the controls handy. Choose Place food and tap a feeding column; Touch hints show your taps. These browser controls require 2.1.1. Version 2.1.2 also fixes the fish-card padlock, sponge/scissors and DONE controls: lock bare-hand cleaning/trimming without disabling explicit tools. The public game is compiled from the latest stable release source, not the ESP32 binary. Its release tag and source commit are shown above the game and checked against the USB installer's version before deployment.

In 2.1.3, a simpler Clock/Weather hub groups Face, Colors, Text / Effects, Time / Zone and Weather, with one Show Clock preview. Weather has its own Small/Medium/Large text control, plus simple condition icons. BACK returns one level, and city removal requires confirmation. Live weather remains a CYD hardware feature; the browser does not contact weather services.

Progress stays in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, private browsing or browser storage eviction can remove it. Hardware-only features are not emulated.

New in 2.1.0

Your scene. Your clock. Comfortable light.

Available in Pocket Tank CYD 2.1.0, alongside all the game features below.

Three scene-detail levels

Normal size and Balanced detail are the defaults. Choose Simple for stationary real seagrass and decorations without helper creatures, bubbles or ambient effects; their benefits continue. Enhanced keeps smooth animations. Size and detail are independent.

Populated-device samples at either size: about 20 FPS Simple, 10 FPS Balanced and 8-9 FPS Enhanced. Results depend on your aquarium.

Jumbo clocks that keep their style

Tiny also joins the size choices. Clock/Weather > Text / Effects has separate Small/Medium/Large controls for date/AM-PM/seconds and weather/activity. Captions wrap and sizes fit down when necessary to prevent overlap at either screen resolution.

Make the time easier to read from across the room. Jumbo respects Minimal, Digital, Analog, Neon, Flip, Binary and Orbit designs, plus your colors, date/seconds options and enabled weather captions. Large, Medium and Small remain available.

Quieter in a dark room

Room-light dimming now controls both the screen and rear LED, with much stronger low-light reduction: about 27% output at half room light, 8% at a quarter, and 2% in darkness. The screen keeps a small usable minimum, while filtering softens brief shadows. Pagination buttons use clear arrows instead of page labels.

Ready to give your CYD a little world?

  1. Check that your board is the supported ESP32-2432S028 CYD or ELEGOO ESP32-32E 2.8-inch ILI9341V, then select it in the flasher.
  2. Use desktop Chrome or Edge and a USB data cable.
  3. Open the flasher and choose a save-preserving update for an existing tank, or a factory installation for a new board.

Back up first. Factory installation overwrites saved tanks and settings. A save-preserving update is a separate operation with its own board/layout checks and confirmation. Nothing flashes from this home page.
