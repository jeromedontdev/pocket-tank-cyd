**Pocket Tank CYD - a more personal little ocean**

I've been expanding a CYD-only fork of Pocket Tank v0.3.8 for the ESP32-2432S028 Cheap Yellow Display. The original feeding, personality, growth, trust, breeding and tank care are still there, with a lot more customization.

**Make each fish your own**
• 10 creature looks: Classic, Angel, Guppy, Tetra, Puffer, Shark, Jellyfish, Stingray, Clownfish and Betta.
• 4 patterns: Stripes, Spots, Band and Plain.
• 24 body colors and 24 marking colors, chosen independently.
• Free customization with an adult preview, Save and Cancel.
• Bred fry inherit appearance traits.

These are peaceful visual varieties using the same care system, not predator/prey simulations. Jellyfish pulse, rays flap their wings, and bettas have flowing fins.

**Make the whole tank colorful**
Sand and water have separate palette controls, including Rainbow, Hot Pink and Neon Lime. Sand also gets Electric Cyan. Original colors remain selectable, and nothing flashes or strobes.

The screenshots are clean captures from the actual desktop renderer, using a separate staged demo tank. They're enlarged with crisp pixels, not webcam photos or mockups.
