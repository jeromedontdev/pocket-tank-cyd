**New: Fish Bank - keep favorites without filling the tank**

The aquarium still has five active fish slots. Keeper > FISH BANK adds **20 separate storage slots**, so making room doesn't have to mean selling a favorite.

**STORE / RETURN / SWAP**
• IN TANK > select a fish > confirm STORE.
• STORED > CHOOSE shows a fish's look, age and trust.
• RETURN brings it back when there's room.
• SWAP trades places with an active fish, even when the tank and bank are both full.

Storage and swaps are free. Keep at least two fish active; swaps work at that minimum too.

**Paused care, preserved identity**
Stored fish don't age, grow, eat or need attention. Their names, colors, patterns, creature looks, stage, age, personality, trust, care state and milestone reward records are preserved. Swimming positions and current goals restart on return.

Every move asks for confirmation and saves immediately. If saving fails, neither fish moves.

The bank is included in internal saves and SD backups. Removing the SD card won't make stored fish disappear. Wipe clears the bank; restoring an older pre-bank snapshot starts with an empty bank.
