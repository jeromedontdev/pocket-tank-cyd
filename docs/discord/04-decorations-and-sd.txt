**Menus that fit the small screen**
Pixel-aligned text, brighter secondary labels, wrapped messages and separate sand/water rows. Settings has Updates, clearly labeled Touch Calibration, and Wipe with a second confirmation. Wipe keeps SD backups and calibration, but clears the aquarium, bank and progression.

**SD backup and restore**
Versioned JSON snapshots include fish, bank, customization, coins, decorations, layout and Journal progress. Backups are read back and validated; restores create a safety snapshot first. Older backup schemas remain importable.

**Offline SD updates**
Settings > Updates checks pocket-tank/update.ptu. It validates the board, image size and checksum, asks for confirmation and makes a pre-update tank backup. Keep power connected.

Packages are local and unsigned: checksums detect corruption, not an untrusted publisher. Only install trusted files.
