**More scenery, a consistent shop and useful SD storage**

**19 added decorations** (sand dollars):
Treasure chest 120; shell/starfish/urn 60 each; anchor/buoy/sea fan 80;
shipwreck 200; crystals 100; ruins 160; jelly lantern 120.
New fixed scenery: pebbles 40, duck 60, pearl clam 70, lily pads 80,

Placeable pieces support MOVE and depth/layering; the eight fixed pieces
do not. Scenery sells back for 20% after two SELL taps.

**Optional upkeep:** Slow Garden 160 halves grass growth (not sword plant
or coral); Bio Filter 180 halves new algae. Both work awake/asleep, only
in their own tank. No free chore rewards or removal of existing growth.
Sand Palette and Mood Lights are 300 each; color cycling is free afterward.

**Cheapest-first shop:** original and added icons are muted gray before
purchase and full color afterward, in the list and detail view. Gray means
unowned, not unaffordable. Dark buttons match across menus; arrows use < >.

**SD backups** include the active tank, bank, Journal, upgrades and shared
wallet. Restores create a safety snapshot first. Wipe clears only the active
tank, keeping other tanks and purchased slots; a multi-tank wallet is kept.
Also archive pocket-tank/tanks; JSON alone is not a whole-collection backup.
