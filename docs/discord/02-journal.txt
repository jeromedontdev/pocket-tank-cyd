**Aquarium Journal - progression without daily chores**

Open Keeper > JOURNAL for Goals, Album and Themes.

**15 Keeper levels and 15 objectives**
Three objectives are active at a time. Tap a completed goal to claim its one-time XP and coin reward, then another takes its place.

Everyday care also earns XP:
• Eaten meal batches: 8 XP
• Cleaned algae colonies: 2 XP
• Every 10 cm of plant trimming: 2 XP
• Successful calm-hold approaches: 10 XP

Spending coins doesn't lower your level. Level 15 starts at 4,200 XP. No daily streaks or absence penalties in the progression system.

**A permanent appearance album**
Record the creature looks you've seen and raised to adulthood. Customizing counts too, and records remain after selling or storing a fish. It's an appearance collection, not a requirement to buy every variety.

**Unlockable backdrops**
• Original: always available
• Kelp Forest: level 3
• Coral Reef: level 6
• Deep Glow: level 10

Backdrops preserve your chosen sand and water colors. Journal progress, album records and the selected theme are included in saves and SD backups.

Screenshots use unlocked demonstration progress, not a fresh tank.
