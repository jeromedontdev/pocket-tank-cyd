**More aquariums, with names and fish-care upgrades**

Keeper > UPGRADES > TANKS offers up to **8 aquariums**. The original is free;
each extra costs **500 sand dollars** from your shared wallet. Buying starts
with two fry and normal naming/customization setup; switching back is free.

**Sand dollars are global.** Fish, decorations, palettes, Journal, a 20-slot
Fish Bank and all care/upkeep upgrades belong to each tank separately.
Inactive tanks are completely paused on SD: no aging, hunger, growth or rewards.
Buying/switching needs SD. Only the active tank lives in RAM/internal storage.
Finish naming new fry before leaving; switches save and verify before committing.

**NAME TANK** edits the active tank's name (15 characters). Select a letter,
use < / >, then SAVE. BACK cancels. Names persist across switches/reboots.

**Four permanent fish-care purchases, per tank:**
- Calm Current 160: energy recovers 25% faster while resting or asleep.
- Cozy Shelter 180: base stress decay is 25% faster, including sleep.
- Play Kit 200: exploring gains curiosity 25% faster.
- Training Ring 240: calm holds add 25% of the normal trust rate.

Training Ring adds to the seahorse bonus (225% total while hitched).
Stats still cap at 10; no bonus stacking, passive trust, extra food rewards,
or changes to hunger/aging/size/breeding requirements.
Exhausted fish also take daytime breathers in seagrass or a resting corner;
Calm Current boosts that recovery too.

Wipe clears the active tank's purchases, fish, bank and Journal, not other
tanks or their slots. With multiple tanks, the wallet is kept. JSON restore
keeps current tank identity/name but replaces the shared wallet.
Archive SD tank files too; erasing NVS loses collection identity.
